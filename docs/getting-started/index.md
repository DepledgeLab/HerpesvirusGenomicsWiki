
## About this Wiki

During the Genomics Satellite Workshop at the 2025 International Herpesvirus Workshop in Berlin, the participants, representing a broad swathe of the herpesvirus community that work
with 'omics data agreed to write a review describing the current state of the field and recommended best practices for the design, execution, and analysis of 'omics based experiments.
Following reviewer feedback, it was decided to split this review into two parts, one focused on the [assembly and analysis of viral genomes](LINK NEEDED) and the other on 
[gene regulation from transcriptomes to chromatin](LINK NEEDED).

The purpose of this Wiki is to provide a companion resource that provides a summary of recommendations and links to tools and workflows that are optimised for specific analyses. 

It is currently maintained by Daniel Depledge (Hannover Medical School) and Adam Whisnant (Hannover Medical School) (note: other contributors to be added later).

If you are interested in contributing to this resource then please contact Daniel Depledge
